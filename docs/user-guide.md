# User Guide

## Who this is for

Freelancers, small business owners, and startup founders reviewing service agreements, NDAs, and similar commercial contracts **without** replacing a lawyer.

## How to use

1. Start the backend and frontend (see `README.md` / `BUILD.md`).  
2. Open the app and go to **Analyze contract**.  
3. Drop or select a **PDF**.  
4. Wait for analysis.  
5. Review:
   - **Contract Overview** — document name, parties, dates, and governing law
     extracted automatically (when found)
   - **Risk distribution and category charts** — share of clauses by risk level,
     and which categories showed up most
   - **Clause cards** — category (one of 48: the 41 official CUAD categories plus
     7 freelancer-relevant additions like Indemnification), confidence, risk,
     plain English, suggested alternative, original text  

## Tips

- Prefer text-based PDFs (not scanned images).  
- Prioritize **HIGH** risk clauses for human review or negotiation.  
- Suggested alternatives are starting points for discussion, not legal drafting advice.  

## Privacy

Uploaded contracts are processed for the request only and are not stored on the server.
